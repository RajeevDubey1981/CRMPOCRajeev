# INDcool WhatsApp knowledge base

Source: https://indcool.in/ · Public catalogue snapshot dated 27 November 2026 (India).

## How to answer customers

- Respond in the customer's language where possible, including English, Hindi or Hinglish. Keep the first reply short and ask one useful follow-up question.
- Match the full model code, capacity, colour/variant and product type before quoting a specification. For ACs, distinguish nominal tonnage and cooling capacity in watts. For freezers, distinguish gross volume and usable storage volume. For water coolers, distinguish storage capacity and cooling rate.
- The model records below reflect public website listings. Confirm current prices, tax, stock, delivery by PIN code, installation charges and offers using the live product page or sales team. A zero price is an enquiry listing. Keep promotional and standard warranties separate. If a product title and its technical details disagree, ask for the rating label or route to sales before confirming that specification.
- Use the official forms for document uploads. Never ask for OTPs, passwords, card details or UPI PINs. Confirm that a service ticket, booking or escalation was created only when the CRM returns a successful result.
- For unavailable specifications, firm quotes, refunds, warranty disputes, dealer onboarding, institutional orders and export enquiries, offer the relevant contact or handoff. Ask for the model, PIN code and order/ticket number only where relevant.
- For smoke, a burning smell, electrical shocks, visible wiring damage or suspected refrigerant leakage, advise the customer to stop using the appliance and contact authorised service. Leave repairs and installation to a technician.

## Orders, service and warranty

- **Order tracking.** Use My Account → Your Orders or the courier tracking details sent after dispatch. The FAQ gives an indicative delivery window of 5–7 business days and says orders can arrive in separate shipments. Confirm the product page and checkout ETA for the customer's PIN code. Report transit damage, missing items or an order marked delivered but not received promptly. The FAQ gives a 24-hour reporting window.
- **Cancellation.** The policy permits requests within 24 hours of ordering, before shipment. After shipment, follow the return process. Provide the order details to info@indcool.in or the helpline. The stated pre-shipment refund window is 7–10 working days to the original payment method. https://indcool.in/cancellation
- **Returns and refunds.** The published return window is 5 working days after delivery for an unused item in its original packaging and condition. Report transit damage within 24 hours with clear photos and a wrong item within 5 working days. Refund processing takes 7–10 working days after receipt and inspection, back to the original payment method. The policy lists shipping charges as non-refundable. Contact support@indcool.in. https://indcool.in/return-and-refund
- **Repair.** Ask for the product category, model or serial, problem, PIN code and invoice or warranty certificate where applicable. The service form accepts PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG up to 2 MB for supporting warranty documents. For ticket tracking use the ticket number and registered mobile number. The ticket page links to the older tracker for requests raised before 19 September 2026.
- **Installation.** The form requests contact and address details, product type, serial number, proof of purchase, dealer name, purchase date and preferred appointment. The date and time are preferences until confirmed. Installation inclusions and charges depend on the applicable offer and site requirements.
- **Warranty.** The published general terms start coverage from the purchase invoice date and apply to the first purchaser in India. For assistance call 1800 119 515 (the warranty page lists Monday–Friday, 10 am–6 pm) or contact the selling dealer. Keep the invoice, warranty certificate where applicable and identification label. The standard terms describe exclusions including tampering, improper installation, abnormal electrical conditions and commercial use. Verify the written terms for the exact model or commercial contract. Compressor-only warranty covers eligible manufacturing defects in the compressor. Labour, refrigerant, transport and related parts may be chargeable under those terms. https://indcool.in/term-and-condition
- **AMC.** The AC Assurance plan lists two preventive maintenance visits and excludes gas charging and spare replacement charges. The AC Comprehensive plan lists covered components and one wet preventive maintenance visit per year. For plan eligibility, exclusions and current tariffs use the AMC page, call 1800 119 515 or email careindcool@gmail.com.
- **Dealer, CSD, bulk and export.** Ask which models or product types are required, quantity, destination/PIN code and required delivery date. For export, ask for country and city. Confirm commercial terms, CSD stock, dealer terms, certificates, MOQ and lead time with the sales team.
- **Product selection.** For AC enquiries, ask for room size, floor, sun exposure, location, budget and whether heating is needed. For a freezer, ask for usable capacity, solid/glass lid and intended stock. For water coolers, ask for users per day, storage, cooling output and whether RO/UV or hot water is needed. For geysers, ask for capacity and mounting orientation. For Wi-Fi, app control, solar and battery backup, confirm the exact model's published specifications.

## Model lookups that need extra care

- **INVERTRO™ versus standard hard-top freezers.** Ask whether the customer wants the battery-backed INVERTRO™ series. The catalogue uses some of the same model identifiers for different product listings. The 300 L INVERTRO™ page states up to six hours of active cooling with battery support, subject to operating conditions. Confirm battery inclusion, configuration and expected backup for the exact quotation. Solar compatibility refers to a suitably configured solar power system. Use the separate 500 L product page for that variant.
- **190 L refrigerator labels.** Some product URLs contain a different star rating from the current page heading. Match the finish and model label, then confirm the BEE label for the unit being sold before quoting a rating.
- **Cooling-only versus heat pump.** Use the model's published operating modes. For example, the Greeno IDCACSBF18K5HC supports heating and cooling. The Dubai Series name alone does not identify a heat pump.
- **Wi-Fi and the INDcool app.** Ask for the complete model before confirming whether app control is supported. Direct pairing or setup issues to the exact model manual or authorised service.
