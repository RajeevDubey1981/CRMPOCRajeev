### Standard installation scope and commercial terms (what installation includes, free installation, extra charges, travel beyond 25 km, spare parts, GST invoice) (part 1 of 2)

Handling note (added by the CRM team): quote the Customer Total (inclusive of 18% GST) and the scope. Prices can change, so tell the customer the final amount is confirmed by the service team.

STANDARD INSTALLATION SCOPE & COMMERCIAL TERMS
1. Split AC standard installation includes IDU/ODU mounting, standard drilling on brick wall up to 10 inches, standard interconnection of supplied piping/cables/drain, basic electrical connection and product demonstration.
2. Window AC standard installation covers standard labour and fitting. Structural cutting, carpentry, masonry, fabrication and other civil work are extra.
3. Extra copper piping, drain pipe, electrical/interconnecting cable, ODU stands, core cutting, welding, scaffolding, protective covers, MCB/stabiliser provision, society permissions and customised work are excluded unless specifically stated.
4. Re-installation does not automatically include dismantling of old stands, old piping, shifting of units or civil restoration.
5. Gas charging is not a routine top-up. Leak diagnosis/rectification, nitrogen pressure testing and proper vacuumisation should be completed before charging. Photographs/videos may be required for warranty approval.
6. Base rates apply within municipal limits or up to 25 km from the authorised service partner's point of operation. Beyond 25 km, additional travel/transport may be charged at Rs. 5 per km beyond the included radius, or actuals where special transport, tolls, parking or remote-area access is required, subject to customer approval.
7. Site inspection / diagnosis may be charged where no repair, installation or approved chargeable work is carried out. If the customer approves the quoted job, the inspection charge may be adjusted against the final service invoice.

### Warranty and gas leakage controls (gas leakage within 90 days of installation, gas charging under warranty, vacuumisation)

Handling note (added by the CRM team): quote the Customer Total (inclusive of 18% GST) and the scope. Prices can change, so tell the customer the final amount is confirmed by the service team.

WARRANTY / GAS LEAKAGE CONTROLS
1. Installation-related gas leakage reported within 90 days of installation shall be investigated by the installing service partner. If leakage is established as an installation workmanship issue, no customer gas-charging labour/refrigerant charge shall be raised for that covered case, subject to INDcool approval.
2. Any gas charging under warranty requires approval from INDcool and supporting photographs/video of the leak, pressure test and vacuumisation where requested.
3. Vacuumisation using a suitable vacuum pump is mandatory before charging refrigerant.
