### INDcool Ice Maker, bullet ice machines: IDCIMM-60, IDCIMM-130, IDCIMM-210 (ice machine, ice maker, bullet ice, commercial ice, cafe, restaurant, bar, hotel)

INDcool Ice Maker, "Pure ice. Every time." Advanced ice making for homes, cafes, restaurants, bars, hotels, offices and commercial establishments. Bullet shape ice machines in three models. Produces up to 120 kg of bullet ice per day (catalogue headline). Made in India. Toll free 1800 11 9515, www.indcool.in.

Models (as printed in the catalogue):
- IDCIMM-60: production 30 Kgs/Day, storage 8 Kgs, 0.22 KW, dimensions H620 x W450 x D500 mm.
- IDCIMM-130: production 60 Kgs/Day, storage 25 Kgs, 0.37 KW, dimensions H815 x W500 x D600 mm.
- IDCIMM-210: production 95 Kgs/Day, storage 26 Kgs, 0.38 KW, dimensions H850 x W675 x D600 mm.
