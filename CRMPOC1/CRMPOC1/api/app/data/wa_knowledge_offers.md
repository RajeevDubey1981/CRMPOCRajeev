### INDcool Free Electricity Offer (scheme, offer, free electricity, electricity bill reimbursement)

What it is (per our website): INDcool pays 100% of the AC's electricity cost, reimbursed monthly, on the eligible 5 Star Inverter Split AC (4800 W cooling, ISEER 5.65). The offer page headline says "Book now at Rs 1", a limited offer that starts 1 January 2027. The product box on the page shows MRP Rs 55,700, discount Rs 5,570, total Rs 50,130 "Free Electricity Offer Included", so the customer must confirm the exact amount payable before buying.
- Cap: up to Rs 2,100 per month (stated for 1.5 Ton ACs).
- Duration: 4 months from the purchase date.
- Eligible: purchases from the official store or authorised dealers; residential electricity connections only, commercial connections are not eligible; eligible models only. T&C apply.
- How to claim: buy and install the AC; upload the monthly electricity bill in the INDcool Connect app or on the website; INDcool verifies the AC's consumption and reimburses to the customer's bank account. The website says claims are processed within 7 days (the FAQ says 7 working days).
- Returns: a return inside the 7-day return window cancels pending claims, and reimbursements already paid are deducted from the refund.

### Payment, EMI, delivery and installation on offer purchases (EMI, payment, cash on delivery, delivery, free installation)

- Payment: cards, UPI, net banking and EMI. No-cost EMI for 3, 6 or 12 months on select cards (the page shows a starting instalment of about Rs 1,530 a month). Cash on Delivery adds Rs 1,500.
- Delivery: shown as free, 3 to 5 business days. Delivery depends on the PIN code, so ask the customer to check their PIN code on the product page.
- Installation: standard installation with up to 3 m of pipe is free. Longer pipe, extra work or accessories may have nominal charges, and the team tells the customer before going ahead.
- Warranty shown on the offer page: compressor 10 years, PCB 1 year, comprehensive 1 year, service across India. For a specific model confirm the warranty from the model's own page.
